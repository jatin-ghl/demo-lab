Tension · one word hides four different flows

“Unsubscribe” is not one interaction

RFC one-click POST

Inbox provider posts directly. No cookies, HTTP auth, redirects, or extra user action. Scope must be encoded in a hard-to-forge recipient/list capability.

Normative

Body-link opt-out

Safe GET previews scope; an explicit POST turns off the named preference. This protects against link scanners while keeping login unnecessary.

Recommended

Preference center

Useful for multiple topics, cadence, and global controls. Re-verify or authenticate before revealing current settings or allowing opt-in.

Broader authority

Authenticated settings

Correct home for re-enable, cross-community changes, identity data, and account navigation. It can follow unsubscribe, but must not block covered marketing opt-out.

What regulations and mailbox providers add

Gmail and Yahoo require or strongly enforce easy one-click behavior for subscribed/marketing mail. CAN-SPAM, CASL, Australia’s Spam Act, GDPR/ePrivacy, and UK PECR apply to commercial or direct marketing—not automatically to every community activity or essential security message. Bottom line

Keep the old email useful. Keep it powerless.

A long-lived link may safely remove one permission. Anything that grants identity, data access, navigation across accounts, or opt-in must use fresh authentication.
